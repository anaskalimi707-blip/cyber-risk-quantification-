- **The Flaw in Traditional CRQ**:
  - Legacy vendors claim: *"Companies that deployed EDR experienced 40% lower ransomware loss."*
  - This is **naive correlation**. Companies deploying EDR also had larger budgets, better engineers, and active patch management.
- **CyberOptix Causal Double Machine Learning (DML)**:
  - Robinson's Orthogonalization ($Y - \hat{\ell}(X) = \theta (D - \hat{m}(X)) + \varepsilon$).
  - Evaluated on 4,200 cross-industry enterprise intervention trials.
  - Separates simultaneous IT rollouts from true control effectiveness.
- **Result**: CFOs stop wasting money on redundant, placebo controls that look good on paper but do not causally reduce exposure.

> **Speaker Notes**:
> "This is our deepest moat. If you ask any legacy tool how much risk a new tool reduces, they give you a correlation. But correlation is not causation. If you roll out an expensive tool at the same time as five other initiatives, legacy tools double-count the benefit. CRIM-X uses Double Machine Learning with cross-fitting to compute the true orthogonalized treatment effect. We tell you exactly how many rupees of risk that specific control will eliminate."
